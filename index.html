A quiet retreat

Designed for rest.
Made for memories.

Villa Alsvik is an exclusive private holiday home in Alsvik, Ryfylke — set on roughly 4,000 m² of garden with a wood-fired hot tub, private sauna, a 14-foot boat, five bedrooms and loft, sleeping up to 15 guests. Just 25 minutes from Preikestolen (Pulpit Rock) and the Lysefjord.
