Chapter II The space

The space

Preikestolen Villa is a spacious private villa for families and groups who want to experience Rogaland properly — with room to breathe, genuine privacy, and everything you need for an unforgettable stay. Set on approximately 4,000 m² of land with manicured gardens, ancient oak trees, a greenhouse, a stream, and multiple outdoor areas. Plenty of space to be together — and room to find your own corner of quiet.

Highlights

  • Wood-fired Skargards Regal 220 hot tub (8–10 guests)
  • Private sauna
  • Fire barrel and three indoor fireplaces
  • Fully equipped kitchen with dining for 12
  • Loft lounge with 65" TV + additional TV on main floor
  • Five bedrooms + loft
  • 14-foot boat with 9.9 hp motor (April–November, by arrangement)
  • Large garden with terraces, seating areas and stream

Sleeping arrangements

Comfortable beds for 12 adults in standard bedrooms. With additional sleeping options, up to 15 guests can be accommodated in total.

Location

Quiet neighbourhood with minimal traffic and excellent privacy — just 25 minutes from Preikestolen and the Lysefjord.

Best suited for

Family holidays, multi-generational trips, groups of friends, and work retreats needing space and tranquility. This villa is best suited for guests who value peace, nature and quality time together — not for events or parties.

Guest access

Guests have full and private access to the entire villa, including the garden, terrace and all outdoor areas. Only a few storage rooms and the garage are locked and not available during the stay.
